Our story

The Chthonic Winery is a Brooklyn-based natural wine project made in collaboration with growers and winemakers upstate — no tasting room, no estate, just a hands-on partnership built around a shared curiosity for how wine was made before modern winemaking gave us shortcuts.

Our focus is ancient technique: flor yeast cultures, oxidative aging under a blanket of olive oil, rancio character coaxed out through controlled light exposure, and botanical preservation methods — thyme, rosemary, pine, elderberry — used the way winemakers relied on them for thousands of years before ready-made sulfites existed.

There is a plethora of these methods to explore, each of which can be tuned to the characteristics of a given varietal and harvest. That wealth of pre-industrial knowledge is a treasure trove of experiments we're excited to run — using the unique flavor profiles that our terroir and native grapes offer to the world of winemaking.
