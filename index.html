Our story

The Chthonic Winery is a Brooklyn-based natural wine project made in collaboration with growers and winemakers upstate — no tasting room, no estate, just a hands-on partnership built around a shared curiosity for how wine was made before modern winemaking gave us shortcuts.

Our focus is ancient technique: flor yeast cultures, oxidative aging under a blanket of olive oil, rancio character coaxed out through controlled light exposure, and botanical preservation methods — thyme, rosemary, pine, elderberry — used the way winemakers relied on them for thousands of years before ready-made sulfites existed.

There is a plethora of these methods to explore, each of which can be tuned to the characteristics of a given varietal and harvest. That wealth of pre-industrial knowledge is a treasure trove of experiments we're excited to run — using the unique flavor profiles that our terroir and native grapes offer to the world of winemaking.

Techniques we work with

Flor Yeast

Native flor cultures develop under oxidative conditions, building complexity without additives.

Olive Oil Reductive Layer

A blanket of extra virgin olive oil applied right after primary fermentation limits oxygen exposure, preserving terpenes and esters.

Rancio via Light Strike

Controlled light exposure encourages rancio character — nutty, aged notes drawn out deliberately rather than avoided.

Botanical Preservation

Thyme, rosemary, and other traditional botanicals stand in for modern preservatives.

Piquette with Pine Needles

Low-intervention second-pressing wines infused with forest character.

Elderberry Co-Ferments

Wild elderberry fruit co-fermented alongside the primary must.

Appassimento

Grapes dried before fermentation to concentrate flavor and sugar.

Dry Barreling

Barrel aging without topping off, encouraging deliberate oxidative development.

Persephone's Boon

2025 Seyval Blanc — Hudson River Region, NY

Made from Seyval Blanc grapes planted in the 1990s and sustainably farmed along the Hudson River in Marlborough, NY. Aged under a blanket of extra virgin olive oil following an ancient minimal-intervention reductive method — a process that fostered the growth of native flor yeast, unlocking tropical notes and umami undertones.

750ml  ·  10% ABV  ·  Contains sulfites
