Ancient techniques. Native vines. Northeast terroir.

A natural wine project exploring pre-sulfite winemaking traditions — flor, oxidative aging, botanical preservation — applied to the hybrid and native grapes of the American Northeast.

Techniques we work with

Olive Oil Reductive Layer

A blanket of extra virgin olive oil applied right after primary fermentation limits oxygen exposure, preserving terpenes and esters.

Piquette with Pine Needles

Low-intervention second-pressing wines infused with forest character.

Demi-Rancio

Controlled light exposure and heat — not the full traditional rancio process — coax out nutty, aged notes deliberately rather than avoiding them.

We're also exploring native flor yeast, botanical preservation (thyme, rosemary, and other traditional preservatives in place of sulfites), elderberry co-ferments, appassimento, and dry barreling — no dedicated products or photos for these yet, so we'll share more as those wines come together.

Current release

Persephone's Boon

2025 Seyval Blanc — Hudson River Region, NY

Made from Seyval Blanc grapes planted in the 1990s and sustainably farmed along the Hudson River in Marlborough, NY. Aged under a blanket of extra virgin olive oil following an ancient minimal-intervention reductive method — a process that fostered the growth of native flor yeast, unlocking tropical notes and umami undertones.

750ml  ·  10% ABV  ·  Contains sulfites
