Ancient techniques. Native vines. Northeast terroir.

A natural wine project exploring pre-sulfite winemaking traditions — flor, oxidative aging, botanical preservation — applied to the hybrid and native grapes of the American Northeast.

Techniques we work with

Olive Oil Reductive Layer

A blanket of extra virgin olive oil applied right after primary fermentation limits oxygen exposure, preserving terpenes and esters.

Piquette

Refermented from Seyval Blanc skins after rehydration with brown sugar, aged six months under flor. Coming this fall in two versions — a straight rendition, and one layered with elderberry (Sambucus nigra), Ceylon cinnamon, and clove, a co-ferment tradition once widespread in Europe before being banned as a wine additive in parts of France and Portugal in the 1800s. Releasing for Halloween/Thanksgiving.

Demi-Rancio

Controlled light exposure and heat — not the full traditional rancio process — coax out nutty, aged notes deliberately rather than avoiding them.

We're also exploring native flor yeast, botanical preservation (thyme, rosemary, and other traditional preservatives in place of sulfites), appassimento, and dry barreling — no dedicated products or photos for these yet, so we'll share more as those wines come together.

Persephone's Boon

2025 Seyval Blanc — Hudson River Region, NY

Made from Seyval Blanc grapes planted in the 1990s and sustainably farmed along the Hudson River in Marlborough, NY. Aged under a blanket of extra virgin olive oil following an ancient minimal-intervention reductive method — a process that fostered the growth of native flor yeast, unlocking tropical notes and umami undertones.

750ml  ·  10% ABV  ·  Contains sulfites

Even Devils Fear What They Cannot Understand

A single varietal — Alicante Bouschet (Garnacha Tintorera) from Contra Costa County, California — carried through five different vinification paths side by side, to see how the same fruit responds to each. That includes our first attempt at the olive oil reductive layer on a red wine, a technique we've so far only run on whites. Not for sale — this batch is purely for comparison.
